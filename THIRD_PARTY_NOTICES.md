license and copyright notices remain in the imported source tree.

## Official Hermes finance skills

CFOAgent activates official optional finance skills distributed with Hermes.
Each copied `SKILL.md` retains its original author, version, and license
metadata. At the time of bootstrap the set includes stock research, Excel and
PowerPoint authoring, DCF, comparable-company analysis, three-statement, LBO,
and merger modeling. These skills identify Apache-2.0 licensing in their
metadata.
